The wedge

Private, proactive, and governed in one system.

The promise is not "AI magic." The promise is that useful autonomy is safer when the person it serves owns the machine, the data, the approvals, and the audit trail.

Private by architecture

Local inference through LM Studio or Ollama. Cloud access is opt-in per agent, and strict-local agents are code-enforced to stay on the machine.

Proactive, not reactive

A self-tasking queue can work around the clock, escalate what needs a decision, and learn which repeat approvals no longer deserve an interruption.

Governed, provably

Every meaningful action passes the risk policy, approval queue, and tamper-evident audit log. The visible product is the control surface, not a staged demo.
