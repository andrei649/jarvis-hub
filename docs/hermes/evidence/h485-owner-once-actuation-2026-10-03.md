## Implemented behavior

The coordinator enrolls the current Telegram transport before terminal intake
can schedule the context-free native judge. A bounded invocation registry binds
the live source, turn, server registration and immutable task identity. It only
prevents premature unattended closure; it grants no approval or execution.
Cancellation before prompt creation releases that registration and settles any
remaining canonical DENY. An unattended invocation retains definitive refusal.

A native DENY now offers the authenticated owner once/reject and waits within
the same ToolRPC invocation. A successful once response yields a private exact
claim. The private worker performs one attempt; ordinary scheduling cannot consume
the row. Off mode verifies the owner receipt; enforced mode also consumes the
existing B7 permit and records the governed execution. Hold and e-stop refuse.
Owner rejection settles without dispatch. Generic controls cannot bypass DENY.

Built-in local, SSH and Docker transports consume the one-use dispatch CAS at
their actual process spawn. They recheck current owner, turn, registration,
deadline, exact request and transport parameters, target policy and kernel floors.
Custom transports and Docker host fallback cannot use owner authority. A paused,
cancellation-resistant transport cannot launch after originating cancellation;
post-dispatch cancellation kills and reaps children without claiming effects were
undone.

The kernel revalidator observes current kill-switch, capability, budget, loop,
policy, taint and exact sealed receipt without consuming B7 or recording another
loop event, audit or metric. Replacement of a bound policy, kill-switch or
capability broker invalidates in-flight authority until rebound. Missing,
awaitable, malformed or mutating runner revalidators refuse execution.

Success requires actual durable DONE, successful outer and nested results and a
distinct exact signed owner completion proof. Synthetic DONE, changed output and
missing proof cannot produce an invocation success. Guardian DENY remains counted
once even after an accepted owner override. Failed owner attempts never retry.
