These are **survivors under the tested conditions**, not claims that each guard is redundant or exploitable. In particular, removing one off-mode guard leaves other signed-mediation checks in place. The no-retry test is part of the baseline, but this campaign did not add an artificial retry implementation merely to inflate mutation totals.

Two mutants are **invalid evidence**: removing per-call remote approval made the test's pre-consent step raise `DataHandlingRefused` rather than its expected refusal, without demonstrating remote use after consent; removing redirect revalidation made the fixture repeat redirects until the redirect limit, without proving a private target reached physical transport. Their exact patches, selectors, and traces remain in [results.json](results.json) and the individual logs. Both need a purpose-built behavioral regression before a kill claim.

The next bounded regression slice should exercise signed worker requests whose URL/method/Authorization/Cookie and direct transport are changed at the physical request seam, a descriptor symlink swap between `FileScope.resolve` and final `os.open`, and a chunked HTTP response without `Content-Length`. A local read-allocation test is useful only if it isolates the resource bound from `fstat` and post-read length checks. No live/provider acceptance or broader Hermes provider-fallback parity is established here.
